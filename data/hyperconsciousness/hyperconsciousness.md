# Hyperconsciousness

Developer-alpha Rust knowledge store for humans and agents, with encrypted append-only records, device sync, scoped expiring grants, and CLI/MCP/HTTP access.

MIT licensed. Grants limit what the server returns; they do not sandbox a process with access to the owner's files or keys. Hosted model providers can see plaintext returned through their grants.
